Reporting Guidelines

Clear reports help authorities assign the right department faster and give neighbors accurate reliability data.

  • State when the service disruption started and how long it has lasted.
  • Include nearby landmarks so field teams can locate the spot quickly.
  • Attach a clear photo when safe to do so (e.g., dry tap, broken lamp, overflow).
  • Save your generated Reference ID to track status and verify resolution.

Our Mission & Purpose

Bridging the Gap Between Promised Services and Reality.

LastMile is designed to give everyday citizens clear visibility into whether essential public utilities—water, electricity, streetlights, and sanitation—actually reach their homes as promised.

The Reliability Gap

Communities frequently face water cuts, voltage drops, dark streets, and irregular waste pickup. Even when schedules are promised on paper, actual last-mile delivery often falls short.

Why Traditional Portals Fall Short

Legacy complaint boxes often close tickets internally without checking whether the citizen still has the problem, hiding recurring neighborhood failures behind averages.

Independent Verification

LastMile separates Resolution Reported by authorities from Verified Resolved by citizens—ensuring real accountability and actionable service analytics.

How Citizens Benefit

  • Simple reporting with photo evidence and unique tracking IDs.
  • Right to confirm or reject resolution claims and reopen unresolved issues.
  • Clear locality reliability comparisons before and after civic action.

How Authorities Benefit

  • Spot recurring failure hotspots instead of treating isolated tickets in isolation.
  • Prioritize high-impact and reopened complaints with structured department routing.
  • Build public trust through transparent performance tracking.
